Civic-Ledger

An as-objective-as-possible source for vetting politicians: what they say
beside what they do, graded on a published formula, plus what Congress is
actually working on this week. Federal first; states later.

Read MISSION.md, then IMPLEMENTATIONv3.md, then STATUS.md.

Stack: Python (uv) ingest -> curation -> data (JSON, committed) -> Astro site
on Cloudflare Workers. Nightly cron refreshes the data.

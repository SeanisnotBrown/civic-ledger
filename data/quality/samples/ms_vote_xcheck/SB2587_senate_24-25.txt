Mississippi State Senate 
2026 Regular Session 
 
 YEAS AND NAYS On S. B. No. 2587.  On motion of Senator England, the rules 
were suspended, the bill considered engrossed, read the third time and, the yeas and 
nays being taken, it failed to pass, title standing as stated, by the following vote: 
 
 Yeas--Barnett, Blackmon, Blount, Boyd, Brumfield, Bryan, Butler, DuPree, 
England, Frazier, Gillespie Isom, Hickman, Hopson, Jackson, Ladner, Mumford, 
Norwood, Pope, Simmons D. T. (12th), Simmons S. (13th), Thomas, Thompson, 
Turner-Ford, Wiggins.  Total--24. 
 Nays--Barrett, Berry, Blackwell, Carter, Chism, DeBar, Fillingane, Hartness, Hill, 
Johnson, Kirby, McCaughn, McLendon, McMahan, Michel, Parks, Rhodes, Seymour, 
Sparks, Suber, Tate, Taylor, Whaley, Williams, Younger.  Total--25. 
 Absent and those not voting--Chassaniol, DeLano, Harkins.  Total--3.

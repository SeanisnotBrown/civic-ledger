### AES-29: one row layout for "what happened" (home, /congress, member timelines)

**What changes.** Every row now reads in the same order: **status and date, then the bill title (bigger, bold), then the government's own action text, then the links** (bill, roll call, official record). Before, each of the three places had its own arrangement and the title was small. Ordering on the home page and `/congress` is untouched (still your "In Congress" order).

- Home and `/congress`: the date now sits beside the status label; the bill number link (e.g. "H.R. 1013 (119th)") moved to the last line.
- Member timelines, vote rows: position, vote type and date on top; bill title big; the question and "Voted to pass it" lines as before; links (bill, "Roll #323", "Official record") on the last line.
- Member timelines, bill-action rows: **these now show the bill's official title** (they only showed the bill number before). This is the one real addition of content. - "Show the full title" controls are now lavender and slightly larger, so they read as controls.
- Statement rows are not touched; they join the pattern later.
